A / The community

Why

London has communities for builders. It has events to show off what you built. It has nothing for researchers to sit down and go deep on one shared idea. So we are making it.

Who

AI researchers in academia, big tech and startups. It is a community, not a sales pitch. Free forever. We curate the room so the right people end up in it. Led by Shubham Jain.

How

Read

Come having read the paper if you can. We share links to summaries, so you know what you are about to hear.

Discuss

We start with the paper. Then the room opens up. Questions, doubts, nitty gritty details.

Go deeper

Seminar room energy. Lots of curiosity. A hunger to push the frontier.

When

We meet once a month, or once every six weeks. Each time: a paper, a discussion, and a room of people who care about the details.
